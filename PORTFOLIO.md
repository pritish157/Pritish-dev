# Target Audience

## Primary — Technical Recruiters

Time on page: 30–90 seconds.

Scanning behavior: headline → projects → tech stack → contact.

Looking for: role match, tech stack alignment, proof of shipped work, easy contact path.

Biggest friction: too much text, unclear role definition, missing contact info, slow page load.

What they need immediately:

- Role and capability in 3 seconds
- Tech stack proof in 10 seconds
- Shipped project evidence in 30 seconds
- Contact method always visible

## Secondary — Hiring Managers

Time on page: 2–5 minutes.

Evaluation behavior: project depth → architecture quality → engineering judgment → communication clarity.

Looking for: problem-solving evidence, system design thinking, trade-off awareness, production readiness.

What they need:

- Case study depth per project
- Architecture decisions explained clearly
- Evidence of end-to-end ownership
- Proof of shipping discipline

## Tertiary — Technical Interviewers

Time on page: 5–15 minutes.

Verification behavior: code quality → system design → technical depth → live demos.

Looking for: credibility markers that generate good interview questions.

What they need:

- GitHub links to real code
- Live demos they can interact with
- Technical detail they can probe in conversation
- Architecture diagrams or explanations

---

# Information Hierarchy

What a visitor should understand at each time threshold:

**3 seconds**: Who (name, role) and what (builds production software).

**10 seconds**: Proof of capability (tech stack, live deployments, availability status).

**30 seconds**: Project depth (featured work with case studies, architecture, outcomes).

**60 seconds**: Full professional picture (experience arc, skill domains, contact channels, resume).

Nothing should block or delay these thresholds.

# Page Goals

The portfolio is a single-page application. Every section has one job.

| Section | Purpose | Recruiter Question Answered |
|---|---|---|
| Navigation | Instant access to any section + resume download | How do I find what I need? |
| Hero | Identity, capability, and primary call-to-action | Who is this? What do they do? |
| Trust Bar | Tech stack proof and availability signal | Do they know the tools we use? |
| Featured Projects | Shipping evidence and engineering depth | Have they built real software? |
| Experience | Career trajectory and professional positioning | Where are they in their career? |
| Skills | Domain breadth organized by engineering discipline | What can they cover? |
| Contact | Easy outreach with multiple channels | How do I reach them? |
| Footer | Navigation, social links, professional closure | Is this a professional? |

If a section does not answer a recruiter question, it should be removed or redesigned.

# Brand Identity

## Positioning Statement

Pritish Kumar Panda is a backend-first engineer who ships polished frontends. The portfolio communicates engineering depth, production discipline, and product craft — not academic knowledge or tutorial completion.

## Tone

Confident without arrogance.

Technical without jargon.

Minimal without emptiness.

Direct without rudeness.

Professional without corporate.

## Design Feel

The portfolio should feel:

- Premium
- Confident
- Minimal
- Technical
- Modern
- Engineering-first

See DESIGN_SYSTEM.md for visual implementation.

## Anti-Patterns

The portfolio must never feel like:

- An AI SaaS landing page
- A startup marketing site
- A crypto or web3 aesthetic
- A template with swapped colors
- An animation showcase
- A technology checklist
- A resume dump
- A clone of another portfolio

# Success Criteria

The portfolio is successful when:

1. A recruiter understands the candidate's value within 30 seconds.
2. A hiring manager finds enough technical depth to schedule an interview.
3. Resume download is accessible within one click from any scroll position.
4. Contact channels are visible without searching.
5. Every featured project links to live code or live demos.
6. The portfolio loads fast on any device and any network.
7. The design feels premium, original, and professional.
8. The portfolio ranks well for name-based searches.
