# Future Feature Ideas & Backlog (`AI/IDEAS.md`)

## 1. Metadata
- **Purpose**: Backlog of potential feature concepts, architectural enhancements, and experimental ideas evaluated for future releases.
- **Owner**: AI Product Manager / Architect.
- **Update Trigger**: Updated when creative enhancement ideas emerge that are out of scope for the current milestone.

## 2. Permitted Contents
- Idea title, category (`NICE_TO_HAVE` / `FUTURE_VERSION` / `EXPERIMENTAL` / `NEVER_BUILD`).
- Brief description and potential value proposition.
- Architectural impact and prerequisites.

## 3. Forbidden Contents
- Unreviewed commitments to active sprints (use `MASTER_PLAN.md`).
- Marketing fluff or buzzword suggestions lacking technical substance.

## 4. Usage Rules for AI Agents
Provides inspiration for future milestones without cluttering current sprint goals.

## 5. Relationship to Core Documents
- **Relationship to `AGENTS.md`**: Enforces focus on current deliverables while preserving future ideas.
- **Relationship to `MASTER_PLAN.md`**: Direct source of items for `MASTER_PLAN.md § Continuous Improvement`.
